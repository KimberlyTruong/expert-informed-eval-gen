Constituents of the systematized concept

Add independent fields for the concept. For each, provide a single value or a comma-separated list of relevant factors. For example, the constituents for misinformation might include "fake controversies" and "prominent political figures". The values for "fake controversies" could be: "death, dating scandal, crime".
